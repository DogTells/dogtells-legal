Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

DogTells respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the information DogTells may collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information you provide

When you create a DogTells account, we may collect your email address and authentication information necessary to create, secure and maintain your account.

When you use DogTells, information associated with your account may include lesson progress, challenge results, challenge scores and analysis history.

2. Dog photos and videos

DogTells allows you to select dog photos and short videos for body-language analysis. Media you choose to submit is processed only when you intentionally request an analysis.

Submitted photos, video frames and related information may be transmitted securely to service providers that DogTells uses to perform artificial-intelligence analysis.

DogTells does not claim ownership of photographs or videos you submit. You should only submit media that you have permission to use.

3. Artificial-intelligence processing

DogTells uses artificial-intelligence services to examine visible canine body-language signals and produce educational explanations. Information submitted for an analysis may therefore be processed by an AI service provider for the purpose of providing the requested DogTells analysis.

AI-generated information can be incomplete or incorrect and should not be considered a veterinary diagnosis, behavioral diagnosis, medical recommendation or guarantee of an animal's future behavior.

4. Account and saved information

DogTells may store information necessary to provide account features, including your email address, completed lessons, challenge activity and saved analysis history.

DogTells currently uses Supabase for services including account authentication and database storage.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and operate DogTells, authenticate your account, save your progress, provide requested analyses, protect the security and reliability of the service, troubleshoot technical problems and improve DogTells.

6. Sharing of information

DogTells does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by technology providers that are necessary to operate the service, such as hosting, database, authentication and artificial-intelligence service providers.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce legal rights, investigate misuse or protect users, animals or others from harm.

7. Data security

DogTells uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data retention

Account information and saved DogTells activity may be retained while your account remains active or while reasonably necessary to provide the service.

Some information may be retained when required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal or regulatory purposes.

9. Deleting your account

You may initiate deletion of your DogTells account from the Account screen. Account deletion is intended to permanently remove your DogTells account and associated saved account data, subject to any information that must lawfully be retained.

10. Children's privacy

DogTells is an educational canine body-language application and is not designed to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

DogTells may update this Privacy Policy as the application, technology or legal requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this policy will be updated when appropriate.

12. Contact

Privacy questions, account-deletion questions and other privacy requests may be directed to DogTells through the official DogTells contact method published with the application.

Account & Data

Delete Your DogTells Account

DogTells users can permanently delete their account directly from inside the DogTells application.

How to delete your account

  1. Open DogTells and sign in to your account.
  2. Open your Account screen.
  3. Scroll to Delete My Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and tap Continue.
  6. Confirm your request by tapping Delete Permanently.

What is deleted

Account deletion permanently removes your DogTells account and associated saved DogTells account data, including saved learning progress, challenge activity and saved analysis history.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you want to use DogTells again after deleting your account, you will need to create a new account.

If you have questions about account deletion or cannot access your account, use the official DogTells contact method published with the application.

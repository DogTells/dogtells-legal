Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

DogTells respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the information DogTells may collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have regarding your information. DogTells is published and operated by Benjamin McGill, a sole trader registered in Australia, and is created with the Board of Canine Behaviorists (BCB), caninebehaviorists.org.

1. Information you provide

When you create a DogTells account, we collect your email address and the authentication information needed to create, secure and maintain your account.

You can explore lessons and games as a guest without an account. Guest progress is stored only on your device.

If you choose to enter your dog's name or a note about what was happening, that text is sent with your analysis request.

Your learning progress, your settings and your agreement to analysis are stored on your device. When you are signed in, a copy of your progress is stored with your account so that it follows you between devices.

2. Dog photos, videos and sound

DogTells lets you take or choose dog photos and short videos for body-language analysis. Media is processed only when you intentionally request an analysis.

For a photo, a resized copy of the image is sent for analysis. For a video, several still frames and the audio track from the clip are sent for analysis.

The DogTells server does not store your photos, videos or audio once the analysis is complete. Our artificial-intelligence provider may keep request data for a limited period under its own data policies. The written result is saved to your analysis history.

DogTells does not claim ownership of media you submit. Only submit media that you have permission to use.

DogTells asks for camera access only when you choose to take a photo or video in the app. When you choose an existing file, your device's own picker is used and DogTells receives only the file you select.

3. Artificial-intelligence processing

DogTells uses artificial-intelligence services provided by OpenAI to examine visible and audible canine signals, to write the analysis, and to turn the written analysis into speech when you ask to listen to it.

Before your first analysis, DogTells asks you to agree to your media being sent to OpenAI. If you do not agree, nothing is sent and the rest of DogTells remains available.

What is sent to OpenAI is limited to the photo or video frames, the sound from a video, the language of the report, and your dog's name and note if you entered them. Your email address is not sent.

AI-generated information can be incomplete or incorrect and is not a veterinary diagnosis, behavioral diagnosis, medical recommendation or guarantee of future behavior.

4. Account and saved information

DogTells stores information needed to provide account features, including your email address, learning progress, game results, your chosen settings and saved analysis history.

DogTells uses Supabase for account authentication and database storage.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and analysis packs are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. DogTells does not receive your payment card details.

DogTells uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases and entitlements. RevenueCat receives an account identifier and purchase information from the store.

6. How information is used

Information is used to provide and operate DogTells, authenticate your account, save your progress, provide requested analyses and audio, count the analyses included with your plan, protect the security and reliability of the service, troubleshoot problems and improve DogTells.

7. Sharing of information

DogTells does not sell your personal information.

Information is processed by technology providers needed to operate the service, such as hosting, database, authentication, purchase and artificial-intelligence providers.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce legal rights, investigate misuse or protect users, animals or others from harm.

8. Data security

DogTells uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention

Account information and saved DogTells activity are retained while your account remains active or while reasonably necessary to provide the service.

Some information may be retained when required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal or regulatory purposes.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your DogTells account from Settings. Deletion permanently removes your account and associated saved data, subject to any information that must lawfully be retained.

If you cannot use the app, you can ask for your account and its data to be deleted by emailing privacy@caninebehaviorists.org from the email address on the account.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel subscriptions through your App Store or Google Play account.

11. Children's privacy

DogTells is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher age that applies where you live, and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be deleted.

12. Your choices and rights

You can use the curriculum, assessments and research library without an account, decline the analysis agreement, change or clear your details in Settings, and delete your account at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to or restrict how it is used. To make a request, use the contact details below.

13. International processing

DogTells and its technology providers operate in more than one country. Your information may be processed in a country other than your own, where privacy laws may differ.

14. Links to other websites

DogTells links to the website of the Board of Canine Behaviorists and to search results for the published studies in the research library. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies. Membership of, or certification by, the Board of Canine Behaviorists is arranged on its website and is separate from DogTells.

15. Changes to this policy

DogTells may update this Privacy Policy as the application, technology or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when appropriate.

16. Contact

Privacy questions, account-deletion requests and other privacy requests can be sent to privacy@caninebehaviorists.org.

This Privacy Policy is also published at dogtells.onrender.com/privacy. Instructions for deleting an account are at dogtells.onrender.com/delete-account.

Delete your DogTells account

You can delete your DogTells account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open DogTells and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm twice. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Email us from the email address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Email: privacy@caninebehaviorists.org or bgkygoodboy@gmail.com

What is deleted

Your account, email address, study record, assessment results and saved analysis reports are permanently deleted. Photos, videos and audio are not stored after an analysis finishes, so there are none to delete.

What is kept

Purchase records held by Apple or Google stay with them. Information that must be kept by law, or for security and fraud prevention, may be retained for as long as that is required.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Deletion cannot be undone.

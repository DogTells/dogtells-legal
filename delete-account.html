Account & Data

Delete Your DogTells Account

DogTells users can permanently delete their account directly inside the DogTells app. If you cannot access the app, you can also request account deletion by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open DogTells and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Account screen.
  3. Scroll to Delete My Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and tap Continue.
  6. Confirm your request by tapping Delete Permanently.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access DogTells, email us from the email address associated with your DogTells account and ask us to delete your account and associated data.

Email DogTells to request deletion

Email: bgkygoodboy@gmail.com

What is deleted

Account deletion permanently removes your DogTells account and associated saved DogTells account data, including saved learning progress, challenge activity and saved analysis history.

Some information may be retained where required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal or regulatory purposes, as described in the DogTells Privacy Policy.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you want to use DogTells again after deleting your account, you will need to create a new account.
